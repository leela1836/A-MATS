An agent that reasons about the market, trades on paper, and learns from how it did — honestly.

A-MATS (Autonomous Multi-Agent Trading System) runs on its own in the cloud, three times a trading day. No human presses go. It screens the whole NSE universe, builds a reasoned plan for the strongest setups, records every decision, and closes the loop by learning from the trades it actually took.

📄 The honest headline first. Extensive walk-forward testing — and now a live paper track record — show A-MATS does not beat buy-and-hold on Indian large- and mid-caps. Timing and stock-selection do not reliably add return over simply holding a basket. This site exists to watch an agent think and learn in the open, and to hold that finding to an honest yardstick — not to sell an edge that markets don't give.

How it keeps itself honest

  • Fair benchmark. Every equity number is shown against an equal-weight buy-and-hold of the same basket, started at the same moment and cash.
  • Real, persistent P&L. The equity curve is built from the journal's realized trade outcomes (each modeled at 10% of starting capital), not a broker that resets — so it can't flatter itself.
  • One position per name. A setup is opened, journaled and resolved exactly once; trades that neither hit their stop nor target expire at market so nothing lingers unrecorded.
  • Survival first. If the paper book draws down past a limit, it stops opening new positions and lives to learn another day.

How it learns

A-MATS has no separate note-store. Its memory is two things: the journal (every decision and how it turned out — its experience) and a small neural-net validator whose weights are the lessons distilled from that experience. As paper trades close, they become training examples; a weekly job folds that lived experience into the gate that vetoes weak setups. Early on it leans on a backtest bootstrap; over time its own history takes over. Honest scope: this sharpens selectivity and survival — it does not conjure an edge an efficient market won't give.

What you're looking at

The live dashboard updates itself as the agent scans — today's activity, open positions with their thesis, the agent-vs-hold equity curve, and what it has learned. The neural-net health page opens up the validator: its weights, feature influence, and an in-browser predictor. Everything is a virtual ₹1,00,000 book — paper trading, not advice.
